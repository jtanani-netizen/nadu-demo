Story

An ode to India, region by region

“Nadu means 'homeland,' an ode to our roots which lie in India… At Nadu we are bringing dishes in their most traditional form to your plate” (official menu). Inspired by the diversity of Indian cuisine, NADU combines authentic ingredients and traditional techniques with contemporary innovation, led by Chef Sujan Sarkar and Head Chef Sanchit Sahu (OpenTable).

  • ✓

    Michelin Bib Gourmand 2025 — awarded in NADU's first year (OpenTable).

  • ✓

    A $60 tasting menu — papad and chutneys, dahi bhalla, then your choice of small plate, large plate, side and dessert (OpenTable diners).

  • ✓

    Every dish, a region — Kerala, Goa, Delhi, Surat, Lucknow, Kolkata, Mangalore — named on the menu itself.
